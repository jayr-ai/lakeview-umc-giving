Youth Connect

A monthly table set for every teen who walks in.

Every month, Youth Connect brings together 100 to 120 teens and volunteers for an evangelistic outreach night reaching barangay youth. It lands on the 4th Saturday of the month, and every single time, it ends the same way: with the gospel shared plainly, and an altar call.

  • Happens every 4th Saturday of the month, reaching barangay youth
  • Draws 100 to 120 teens and volunteers each time
  • Always ends with the gospel shared, and an altar call

What your partnership provides

₱4,000 a month - ₱48,000 a year - covers food for every teen who walks through the door - a full table, every time, for every teen.
