Leaders & Volunteers Appreciation Night

One night a year, for the people who give the rest of theirs.

Once a year, we set aside a night to honor our unpaid volunteer leaders - the people who give their weeknights and weekends to disciple others, on top of their own jobs and families.

  • Happens once a year
  • Honors leaders who serve entirely as volunteers
  • A small thank-you for people who give far more than they're asked

What your partnership provides

₱10,000 a year - ₱10,000 a year - is set aside for one night that honors the people who give the rest of their year to everyone else.
